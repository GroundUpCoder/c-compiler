## The os-doom failure (evidence so far)

- `waitFrame` times out with an IDENTICAL region signature across two
  independent runs: `{"h":2417206421,"colors":4,"nonTeal":1022,"n":50032}`
  — a static teal-plus-icons screen, i.e. doom's window never composited
  into the sampled region during the poll window. Identical hashes mean
  this is NOT the known os-doom "load flake" class.
- Doom itself is FINE in the browser: `zz-doom-debug.mjs` drives the
  same page the same way (`doom &` at VT1 → `__osVtSwitch(2)` → wait)
  and gets a fully composited frame — region stats
  `{"colors":111,"nonTeal":61936,"n":61936}` — and `wmctl list` shows
  `DOOM Shareware 640x400+12+36`. Headless doom
  (`test_os_apps_e2e.js`) is also green.
- The tell: the failing runs sample `n=50032` points where the region
  (16,40)-(648,432) must yield `632*392/4 = 61936`. The test's
  `region()` snapshot ran against a canvas of unexpected dimensions —
  i.e. a test-side race with the VT2 screen re-mode (0023's viewport
  tracking) rather than an OS-side regression is the leading theory.
  os-doom's canvas-commit guard (`r.width == __osScreen.w`) may pass on
  a stale value, or the drawImage snapshot races the OffscreenCanvas
  resize.
