## Goal

Build ReactOS **solitaire** and **spider** as C++ apps on the win32 veneer via
clang — **Tier 4** of the existing C++ port ladder, and **the first C++-on-veneer
rung**. Everything below Tier 4 is already green (Box2D + ImGui at Tier 1, ETL +
GLM at Tier 2, Ninja + tinyrenderer at Tier 3, plus Stockfish); what has never
been proven is C++ against the **win32 veneer**.

These two apps are chosen because they are the **cheapest possible first rung**:
they use `cardlib` plus plain win32 imports, with **no ATL** and **no exceptions
requirement**. That keeps this ticket about *C++ meeting the veneer* and nothing
else. ATL is deliberately the *next* ticket (0456).

## Blocked by 0347

`todos/0347-win32-veneer-apps-clang-buildable.md` — cc2wasm cannot currently

overlay). **Every** ReactOS app is a win32 app, so 0347 gates this entire
category. Its scoping already flags a hidden FreeType leg.

⚠️ **0347 is already open at P3 and jku said to leave its status and priority
as-is** unless there is a positive reason to change them. Do not reprioritise it
as a side effect of this ticket.

The scoping pass reported these; @master **could not verify them**, because

⇒ **If either premise is false, STOP and report it rather than routing around

disproves its ticket's premise has done the expensive half of the work; the
resolution must be re-derived against the *goal*, not patched to keep the gate
green.)

## Plan

1. Verify the two premises above; report.
2. Vendor solitaire + spider and `cardlib` following the existing ReactOS port
   precedent (`vendor/notepad/`, `vendor/winmine/`, `vendor/calc/`, each with a
   `bin.json` **and** an entry in `os/win32/ports.json`).
3. Build them with clang against the veneer, on top of 0347.
4. Ship as **optional `*-clang` gucman packages** per the established
   convention — measured: **9** such packages exist today (`box2d-clang`,
   `cpython-clang`, `doom-clang`, `etl-clang`, `gameboy-clang`, `glm-clang`,
   `imgui-clang`, `ninja-clang`, `tinyrenderer-clang`). The base image stays
   clang-free.

2. **Both apps build and run** on the veneer via clang, and are playable.
3. **Registered in `os/win32/ports.json`** with `PORTS.md` regenerated by
