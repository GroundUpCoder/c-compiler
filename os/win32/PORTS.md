win32 apps also build IN-OS (the source-lib design, Lane B2): `cc app.c`
with `#include <windows.h>` pulls the whole veneer + freetype through the
header's `__require_source` block; wWinMain apps compile
`cc -DUNICODE app.c /usr/src/win32/wwinmain.c` (the CRT shim stays an
explicit per-app TU). On a minimal image, `gucman install win32` plants
the headers/sources under /usr/local/{include,src} first; the fat image
bakes them at /usr/{include,src}. `.res` resource sidecars remain
host-only (tools/win32rc.js — an in-OS rc compiler is a follow-on).
